📐 Physics & Theory

Cantilever Beam

Fixed at one end, free at the other. Load P applied at the free end.

δ(x) = (P·x²)/(6EI) · (3L − x) δ_max = PL³ / (3EI) σ_max = P·L·c / I
  • Maximum deflection at the free tip
  • Maximum stress at the fixed wall
  • Bending moment: M(x) = P(L − x)

Simply Supported Beam

Supported at both ends. Point load P at the centre.

δ(x) = (P·x)/(48EI)·(3L² − 4x²) [x ≤ L/2] δ_max = PL³ / (48EI) σ_max = P·L·c / (4I)
  • Maximum deflection at the midpoint
  • Maximum stress at the centre
  • Reactions: R_A = R_B = P/2

Key Variables

  • E — Young's modulus (material stiffness)
  • I — Second moment of area (cross-section shape)
  • c — Distance from neutral axis to outer fiber
  • L — Beam length
  • P — Applied point load
  • σ_y — Yield strength (material limit)

Cross-Section Properties

Rectangular: I = b·h³/12, c = h/2 Circular: I = π·d⁴/64, c = d/2 I-Beam: I = 2·I_flange + I_web

A larger I (deeper or wider section) drastically reduces deflection and stress — this is why I-beams are used in construction!
